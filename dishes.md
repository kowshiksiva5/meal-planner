### Which wets work with roti

Soya curry, sweet potato curry, paneer butter masala, egg drop curry, chicken
curry, chole, bobbarlu curry, gobi tomato curry, chikkudukaya tomato curry.
Charus, pulusus, sambar and the milk curries are too thin for roti, so pair
them with a dry that carries the roti.

Plan from this list only. Don't add outside dishes unless asked.
