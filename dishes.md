# Dishes

The master list. Everything the plan draws from.

## Fixed anchors

These do not rotate. They hold their slot every week.

| Slot | Dish | Base |
|---|---|---|
| Midweek (Wed, or Thu when shifted) | Chicken ghee roast + majjiga charu | White rice |
| Saturday | Chicken fry + chicken curry (tight) | Bagara rice |
| Friday — chapathi day | Egg bhurji + one paneer | Chapathi |

Friday's paneer alternates: paneer butter masala ↔ kaju paneer.
